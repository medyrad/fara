When implementing this Design System in Figma Make, prioritize in this order:

1\. RTL architecture  
2\. IranSansX font  
3\. Color system  
4\. Typography  
5\. Grid  
6\. Spacing  
7\. Navigation  
8\. Hero  
9\. Major dark sections  
10\. Investment fund components  
11\. Content architecture  
12\. Responsive behavior  
13\. Motion  
14\. Micro-interactions

Do not sacrifice hierarchy or usability for visual effects.

The final result must look like a premium, modern, institutional financial website with a highly polished digital experience.

The visual reference should guide the overall composition, rhythm, typography scale and premium minimalism.

The Farasood brand identity must remain distinct through its primary orange \#F4512A and secondary dark \#1B1E22.
